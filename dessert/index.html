Blackberry Cobbler

Filling Ingredients

  • 3–4 cups blackberries
  • ¾ cup sugar (more if berries are bitter)
  • 3–4 T flour

Crust Ingredients

  • 1¾ cups flour
  • 3 T sugar
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ cup shortening
  • ½ cup whipping cream
  • ½ cup buttermilk
  • Melted butter + sugar for topping

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425°F. Mix berries, sugar, and flour in a Dutch oven or 8×8 casserole dish. Make the topping by combining first four dry crust ingredients, then cutting in the shortening with a pastry blender until crumbly. Stir in whipping cream and buttermilk and mix until just blended. Place spoonfuls closely together on top of the berries. Paint with melted butter and a little sugar. Bake uncovered for 30 minutes. Serve with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream — or both.

Brownie Cut Out Cookies

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup butter, softened (1 stick salted, 1 stick unsalted)
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅔ cup unsweetened cocoa (60 grams)

Instructions

Whisk the flour, salt and baking powder in a bowl and set aside. Mix the butter, sugar, eggs, vanilla and cocoa in a mixer. Gradually add the flour mixture and mix until smooth. Wrap in plastic and chill for at least one hour.

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Roll out the dough on a floured counter. Cut into desired shapes, brushing extra flour off the top.

Bake on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 8 to 11 minutes (8 for ⅛-inch cookies, 11 for ¼-inch cookies), until the edges are firm and the centers are slightly soft and puffed. Ice when done.

Note: Becky found this recipe on Pinterest, and we started making them every year for Halloween.

Lemonade Pie

Ingredients

  • 2 pkgs vanilla pudding mix
  • 6 oz lemonade concentrate
  • 1¾ cups whole milk
  • 8 oz Cool Whip
  • 1 graham cracker pie crust

Instructions

Whisk first 3 ingredients together, then fold in the Cool Whip. Pour into pie crust, refrigerate, and enjoy.

Note: This one came from David and Sally in Mexico Beach Florida.

Mrs. James' Apple Pie

Crust

  • 1 recipe Mrs. James' Pie Crust

Crumb Topping

  • ¾ cup flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ⅓ cup cool butter

Filling

  • 5 large apples (about 2½ lbs)
  • 4 tsp lemon juice
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 1 T tapioca

Instructions

Crumb topping: mix the flour, ½ cup sugar and cool butter together until it resembles crumbs.

Filling: mix the apples, lemon juice, granulated sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt and tapioca together and place in the pie shell. Top with the crumb topping.

Bake 10 minutes at 450°F, then reduce the oven to 350°F for 40–60 minutes. The juices should be bubbling.

Note: This makes one regular pie. If making two pies at once, double the topping and filling — the crust recipe makes enough for two pies.

Mrs. James' Pie Crust

Ingredients

  • 2 generous cups flour
  • 1 scant tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup ice-cold water
  • ¾ cup Crisco

Instructions

Mix the flour and salt. Remove ½ cup of the flour mixture to a separate bowl and mix it with the ⅓ cup ice water to make a paste.

Cut the Crisco into the big bowl of flour. Mix the paste into the flour and Crisco, then mix with your hands into a ball.

Separate the ball into two halves and roll out between parchment paper. Makes two crusts. If you want to freeze it, roll it out and place it in the pie pan before freezing.

Note: Any leftover crust can be rolled out, topped with cinnamon sugar, and baked for an extra treat.

Oatmeal Creme Pie Cake

Oatmeal Cookies

  • 2 sticks salted butter, room temperature
  • 1¾ cups packed dark brown sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups quick-cooking oats
  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour

Marshmallow Buttercream

  • 2 sticks salted butter, room temperature (for the buttercream)
  • 14 oz marshmallow fluff
  • 2 cups powdered sugar

Instructions

Buttercream: in a stand mixer, mix the butter on medium until smooth. Add the marshmallow creme and mix on medium until smooth. Turn to low and slowly add the powdered sugar until combined, then beat on medium until smooth.

Cookies: preheat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. In a stand mixer, mix the butter and brown sugar on medium for 2 minutes. Add the eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, baking soda and salt and mix on medium until smooth. Turn to low, add the oats and flour, and mix until combined.

Using a 2 T cookie scoop, drop the dough 2 inches apart on the baking sheet. You should have 35 cookies. Bake 8 to 9 minutes, until golden around the edges. Don't overbake. Transfer the cookies to a cooling rack.

Assemble: divide the cookies into 5 equal portions and the buttercream into 5 equal portions. Arrange one portion of cookies on a cake stand and top with buttercream. Repeat, ending with buttercream.

Note: From Cookies and Cups.

Red Velvet Cake

Cake Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • Pinch salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 T white vinegar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 T natural cocoa powder
  • 2 T red food coloring
  • 1½ sticks unsalted butter, softened
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar

Frosting Ingredients

  • 2 sticks unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1½ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Instructions

Cake: Heat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour two 9-inch cake pans. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl. Whisk buttermilk, vinegar, vanilla, and eggs in a large measuring cup. Mix cocoa and food coloring in a small bowl until a paste forms.

Mix butter and sugar on medium speed about 2 minutes until fluffy. Add ⅓ of the flour mixture and mix. Add half the liquids and mix. Add half the remaining dry ingredients and mix. Add remaining liquid and mix. Add last of the dry ingredients and mix. Add cocoa mixture and incorporate. Pour into prepared pans. Bake 25 min., or until a toothpick comes out clean. Allow to cool 10 min. before removing from pans. Let cool at least 30 minutes.

Frosting: Beat butter and sugar on medium-high until fluffy. Add cream cheese. Beat in vanilla. Refrigerate until ready to use.

Variation — Cake Balls: Once the cake is cooled, crumble it (with a little cream cheese frosting for glue) into bite-sized balls and dip into melted dipping chocolate. Refrigerate and enjoy.
Note: This recipe is from Becky's mom. Most people acknowledge it's the best red velvet cake they've ever had.
