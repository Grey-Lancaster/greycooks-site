Sausage Breakfast Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 lb breakfast sausage (mild or hot)
  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • 4 cups frozen hash browns (shredded)
  • 2 cups grated cheddar cheese, grated from a block (plus a little extra for the top)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
  2. Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium heat. Drain any excess grease.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.
  4. Stir in the hash browns, cooked sausage, and 2 cups of grated cheese.
  5. Pour into the prepared baking dish and spread evenly. Sprinkle a little extra cheese on top.
  6. Bake for 45–50 minutes, until set and golden brown.

Why grated? Grate the cheese yourself from a block. Do not use bagged shredded cheese. Bagged cheese is coated with anti-caking starch or cellulose (wood pulp) so the shreds do not clump in the bag. That coating keeps the cheese from melting smoothly and can leave the casserole grainy or greasy. A block has no coating, so freshly grated cheese melts right into the eggs and milk. Always grate it yourself.

Papa Lanc's Tip: Make it the night before, cover, and refrigerate. In the morning, just pop it in the oven and let it bake while the coffee brews!
