Stokes' Family Tater Casserole

Scalloped Potatoes (Cheesy Mushroom Soup Casserole)

Ingredients

  • 6 medium potatoes (or 3 large), sliced thin
  • 2 medium onions, sliced
  • 5 tbsp butter (2 for the onions, 3 for layering)
  • 1 bar (8 oz) sharp cheddar cheese, grated
  • 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
  • ½ soup can of milk
  • Salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Boil the sliced potatoes in salted water until tender.
  2. Sauté the sliced onions in 2 tbsp butter.
  3. Grease a 9x13 Pyrex dish.
  4. Layer the potatoes and onions, then 3 tbsp thinly sliced butter, then the cheese.
  5. Cover with the soup and milk.
  6. Salt and pepper.
  7. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes.

Shortcut (no boiling, no sautéing): Layer the raw sliced potatoes and onions in the greased 9x13 Pyrex dish. Dot with the 3 tbsp of butter before the cheese layer. Cover with the soup and milk, salt and pepper, and bake covered for 1½ hours.

Notes:
  • Grate an 8 oz bar of sharp cheddar cheese. Do not use bagged. The anti-caking coating keeps it from melting right. Always grate it yourself.
