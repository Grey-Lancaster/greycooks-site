QUICHE

Printed from COOKS.COM

3 eggs

1/4 c. melted butter

1/2 c. Bisquick mix

1 1/2 c. milk

1/4 tsp. salt

1/4 tsp. pepper

1 c. Swiss cheese, grated

1 c. broccoli

1/2 c. bacon or ham

1/2 c. mushrooms

Mix the eggs, Bisquick, milk, salt and pepper together. Pour mixture

into a quiche dish. Then add the cheese, broccoli, ham or bacon and

mushrooms. Bake at 350 degrees for 45-50 minutes or until brown on

top. Easy to make, delicious to eat.
