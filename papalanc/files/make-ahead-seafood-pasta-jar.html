Make-Ahead Seafood Pasta Jar

Prep-ahead creamy white sauce base with raw whole shrimp for fresh poaching

Ingredients

  • Whole Raw Shrimp: 1/2 lb (peeled, deveined)
  • Minced Clams: 1 can (6.5 oz), drained (reserve juices!)
  • Heavy Whipping Cream: 1 cup
  • Seafood Stock: 1 cup
  • Reserved Clam Juice: 1/2 cup
  • Celery: 1/2 cup, finely shredded or minced
  • Butter: 2 tbsp
  • All-Purpose Flour: 1 tbsp (essential stabilizer)
  • Fresh Lemon Juice: 1–2 tsp
  • Pasta: 8 oz (Linguine or Fettuccine, cooked fresh to serve)

Phase 1: Cook & Cool Sauce Base

  1. Sauté Celery: Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté celery for 3 minutes until soft.
  2. Build Roux: Sprinkle flour over celery. Stir constantly for 1 minute to cook off raw flour flavor.
  3. Reduce Liquids: Slowly whisk in seafood stock and reserved clam juice. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until reduced by half.
  4. Thicken with Cream: Stir in heavy cream. Bring to a gentle simmer for 5 minutes until it coats the back of a spoon. Remove from heat and stir in lemon juice.
  5. Chill Completely: Allow the sauce to cool completely to room temperature before assembling the jar.

Phase 2: Mason Jar Assembly (Bottom to Top)

  • Layer 1 (Bottom): Pour the completely cooled cream and celery sauce base into a clean wide-mouth quart mason jar.
  • Layer 2 (Middle): Drop the drained minced clams directly on top of the cream layer.
  • Layer 3 (Top): Place the raw, whole shrimp at the very top, keeping them clear of the liquid.

Seal tightly and store in the refrigerator for up to 24–48 hours.

Phase 3: Heat, Poach & Serve

  1. Empty Jar: Pour the entire contents of the jar (sauce, clams, and raw shrimp) directly into a cold skillet or saucepan.
  2. Gently Melt: Warm over medium-low heat, stirring frequently. Do not use high heat, as gentle warming prevents the cream from separating.
  3. Poach Shrimp: Once the sauce reaches a low simmer, cook for 3 to 5 minutes. The whole raw shrimp will poach right in the bubbling cream, turning pink and perfectly tender.
  4. Toss with Pasta: Toss freshly boiled, hot pasta straight into the skillet with the bubbling sauce for 30 seconds before plating.

Enjoy your fresh-cooked gourmet meal prepared ahead of time!
