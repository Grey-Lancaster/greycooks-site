Golden Yellow Cornbread

GOLDEN YELLOW CORN BREAD OR CORN MUFFINS

1 cup Indian Head Yellow Corn Meal
1 cup all-purpose flour
½ cup sugar
1 teaspoon salt
2 teaspoons baking powder
1/4 cup oil

4 tbs butter
2/3 cup milk
2 egg, beaten

Corn Bread:
Preheat oven to 375 degrees.

Step 1: Preheat the Oven & Skillet (The Butter Trick)

  1. Place your 9.5-inch cast iron skillet directly into the oven.
  2. Turn the oven on to 375°F. Let the skillet heat up right along with the oven.
  3. When the oven is almost fully preheated, drop the 4 tablespoons of butter into the hot skillet.
  4. Let the butter melt completely and bubble. Watch it closely so it doesn't burn. It should look golden and smell rich.

Step 2: Begin the Mixing Process

While the butter finishes melting in the oven, start your batter:

  1. Beat the eggs: Crack your eggs into your mixing bowl and whisk them completely first.
  2. Add the dry ingredients and oil: Dump in your cornmeal, flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, and the 1/4 cup of oil. Stir this together until the ingredients are fully combined. It will look like a thick, crumbly paste at this stage.

Step 3: Incorporate the Hot Butter

  1. Carefully remove the screaming-hot skillet from the oven using a thick oven mitt.
  2. Swirl the melted butter around so it completely coats the bottom and halfway up the inside walls of the skillet.
  3. Pour the butter into the bowl: Immediately pour almost all of the melted butter straight from the skillet into your mixing bowl. Leave a thin, shiny layer of melted butter behind in the skillet to keep it greased.
  4. Quick-stir the hot butter into your egg and flour mixture until smooth.

Step 4: Add the Milk Slowly & Bake

  1. Stream the milk: Pour your milk in slowly, a little bit at a time, stirring as you go.
  2. Check the consistency: Stop adding milk as soon as the batter reaches a smooth, pourable consistency (like thick pancake batter). If it looks right, stop—do not feel pressured to use all the milk if it risks making it too runny.
  3. Sizzle and bake: Immediately pour your finished batter into the hot, butter-coated skillet. It should sizzle beautifully around the edges.
  4. Pop it straight into the 375°F oven and set your timer for 20 minutes

Bake for 20-25 minutes.

The 3-Minute Honey Butter Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, completely softened to room temperature
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons powdered sugar (this adds a distinct diner-style fluffiness, but you can use extra honey instead)
  • 1 pinch of fine salt (skip this if you only have salted butter on hand)

Instructions:

  1. Mash the butter: Place your softened butter in a small bowl and mash it with a fork or a small whisk until it is completely smooth and creamy with no lumps.
  2. Add the sweetness: Pour in the honey, powdered sugar, and a pinch of salt.
  3. Whip it: Whisk or stir vigorously for about 1 minute until the honey is fully incorporated and the butter looks pale, light, and fluffy.

Flavor Variations to Try

  • Cinnamon Honey Butter: Stir in 1/4 teaspoon of ground cinnamon for a warm, cozy flavor profile.
