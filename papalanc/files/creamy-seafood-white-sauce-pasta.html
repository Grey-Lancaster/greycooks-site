Creamy Seafood White Sauce Pasta

A rich, velvety seafood reduction featuring shredded shrimp, minced clams, and celery

Prep Time: 15 mins • Cook Time: 20 mins • Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Pasta (such as Fettuccine or Linguine)
  • 1/2 lb Shrimp, peeled, deveined, and finely shredded or chopped
  • 1 can (6.5 oz) Minced Clams, drained (juices reserved!)
  • 1 cup Seafood Stock
  • 1/2 cup Reserved Clam Juice (from the can)
  • 1 cup Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 2 stalks Celery, finely minced or shredded
  • 2 tbsp Butter
  • 1 tbsp All-Purpose Flour (optional, for extra thickness)
  • 1-2 tsp Fresh Lemon Juice
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh chopped parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water, and set aside.
  2. Sauté the Base: Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced celery and sauté for about 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent. (If using flour for a thicker sauce, sprinkle it over the celery now and stir continuously for 1 minute).
  3. Reduce the Stock: Pour in 1 cup of seafood stock and 1/2 cup of the reserved clam juice. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat slightly and let the liquid simmer down until reduced by half (about 5 to 7 minutes). This concentrates the briny ocean flavors.
  4. Build the Cream Sauce: Pour in 1 cup of heavy whipping cream. Adjust heat to maintain a gentle, low simmer. Let the sauce reduce and naturally thicken for about 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Fold in Seafood: Turn the heat down to low. Fold in the shredded shrimp and drained minced clams. Stir gently and let them poach in the hot cream base for just 2 to 3 minutes until the shrimp turn pink and opaque. Do not overcook.
  6. Combine and Finish: Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir in 1 to 2 teaspoons of fresh lemon juice. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste. Toss the hot cooked pasta directly into the skillet, coating it thoroughly with the sauce. If the sauce is too thick, splash in a little reserved pasta water.
  7. Garnish and Serve: Plate immediately and garnish with a generous handful of fresh chopped parsley.
Chef's Pro Tip: Shredding the shrimp ensures that every single forkful of pasta gets coated in succulent seafood flavor, perfectly mirroring the texture of the minced clams. Be sure to pull the sauce off the heat immediately once the seafood finishes poaching to keep it tender!
