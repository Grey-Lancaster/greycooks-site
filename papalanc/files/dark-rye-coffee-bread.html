Dark Rye Coffee Bread (Bread Machine Recipe)

(7 oz coffee version)

Ingredients

Liquids

  • 7 oz brewed coffee, cooled
  • 2 ½ Tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 ½ Tbsp dark molasses

Dry Ingredients

  • 263.5 g bread flour
  • 121.1 g medium rye flour
  • 6.6 g granulated sugar
  • 9.9 g salt
  • 8.2 g cocoa powder
  • 2.0 g onion powder
  • 7.4 g active dry yeast (Red Star)

Only the ingredient list is saved with this recipe. Add liquids first, then the dry ingredients, and use your bread machine's normal dark or rye cycle.
