Blackberry Cobbler

Blackberry Cobbler

3-4 cups blackberries

¾ Cups sugar (maybe a little more if the berries are bitter)

3-4 Tablespoons of flour

Crust

1 ¾ cups flour

3 T sugar

1 ½ tsp baking powder

¾ tsp salt

¼ cup shortening

½ cup whipping cream

½ cup butter milk

Preheat oven to 425. Mix berries, sugar, and flour in dutch oven or 8 x 8 casserole dish. Make topping by combining first four ingredients then cutting in the shortening with a pastry blender until crumbly. Stir in whipping cream and buttermilk and mix until just blended. Place spoonfuls closely together on top of berries. Paint with melted butter and a little sugar. Bake uncovered 30 minutes. Serve with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream or both.
