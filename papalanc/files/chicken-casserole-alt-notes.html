Chicken Casserole (alt notes)

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1 can asparagus
  • 2 cream of chicken (soup)
  • ½ pt. sour cream
  • 1 cup mayo.
  • 1 cup sharp cheese, grated
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 t. lemon juice
  • 1 t. curry powder

Directions

  1. Cook chicken. Lay canned asparagus on top.
  2. Mix in bowl all other ingredients except the grated cheeses.
  3. Pour mixture on top. Sprinkle heavily grated sharp cheese on top. Then sprinkle parmesan cheese on top of that.
  4. Bake 30 minutes 350 degrees.

Why grated? Grate the cheese yourself from a block. Do not use bagged shredded cheese. Bagged cheese is coated with anti-caking starch or cellulose (wood pulp) so the shreds do not clump in the bag. That coating keeps the cheese from melting smoothly. A block has no coating, so freshly grated cheese melts right in. Always grate it yourself.
