Cajun Cream of Shrimp Soup Mix

Yield: About 3 cups of dry mix (replaces about 9 cans of condensed soup)
Style: Louisiana Creole & seafood base

Ingredients

  • 2 cups nonfat dry milk powder (1½ pouches from a 9.6 oz box)
  • ¾ cup cornstarch
  • 4 large Knorr shrimp bouillon cubes, crushed fine
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon Louisiana crawfish seasoning (salt omitted from mix)
  • ½ teaspoon dried dill weed (or dried oregano)
  • ½ teaspoon coarse black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon smoked paprika

Preparation & Storage

  1. Crush the cubes: Unwrap the 4 shrimp bouillon cubes and smash them with a fork or your fingers until they're a completely fine, lump-free powder.
  2. Combine: In a medium mixing bowl, combine the crushed bouillon powder with the dry milk, cornstarch, and all remaining spices. Whisk thoroughly until uniform in color.
  3. Store: Transfer to an airtight jar or container. Keep in a cool, dark, dry place like the pantry. Use within 6 months.

Cooking Directions (to replace 1 can of soup)

Use this whenever a casserole, pasta sauce, or chowder recipe calls for a 10.5 oz can of condensed cream soup.

  1. Measure: Scoop ⅓ cup of the dry mix into a small saucepan.
  2. Liquid: Whisk in 1 cup of cold liquid (water, whole milk, or seafood stock) until completely smooth.
  3. Thicken: Place over medium heat, stirring constantly with a whisk. Bring to a gentle boil and simmer 1 to 2 minutes until thick, creamy, and glossy.
  4. Shrimp twist: Stir in a handful of cooked salad shrimp or sautéed holy trinity (diced onion, celery, bell pepper) for a quick, robust meal.
