Brown Rice

Brown Rice

1 can beef consume

1 can beef bullion

1 can white rice

2 Strips bacon

1 Onion

1 small can mush rooms

Fry bacon and crumble. Sautee chopped onion in bacon grease. Mix ingredients in a casserole dish. Bake at 350 until liquid is absorbed. Probably 30 minutes.
