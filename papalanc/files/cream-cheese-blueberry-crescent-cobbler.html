Cream Cheese Blueberry Crescent Cobbler

Prep: 15 minutes  ·  Cook: 30–35 minutes  ·  Servings: 8–10  ·  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (8 oz each) refrigerated crescent roll dough sheets or triangles
  • 2 cans (21 oz each) blueberry pie filling
  • 1 block (8 oz) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar (for the cream cheese layer)
  • 1 large egg (helps bind and set the cream cheese layer)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted (for the top)
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon-sugar (optional, for sprinkling)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Roll out the bottom crust using the first can of crescent dough. Press it evenly into the bottom of the dish, firmly pinching any perforations together to create a solid, seamless sheet.
  3. Make the cream cheese filling by beating the softened cream cheese, ¼ cup of sugar, the egg, and the vanilla extract in a medium bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and completely free of lumps.
  4. Layer the fillings by gently spreading the cream cheese mixture directly over the bottom layer of crescent dough. Carefully spoon the blueberry pie filling evenly over the top of the cream cheese.
  5. Add the top crust by unrolling the second can of crescent dough. Gently lay it on top of the blueberry layer, stretching it to reach the sides. Pinch the outer edges of the top and bottom dough layers together to seal everything in.
  6. Finish the top by brushing the 2 tablespoons of melted butter evenly over the top dough, then dust with the cinnamon-sugar.
  7. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes. The top should be a deep golden brown, and you should see the blueberry filling bubbling quietly at the edges.

Critical Baking Adjustments

  • Oven temperature lowering: Unlike standard crescent cobblers that bake quickly at 375°F or 400°F, a dense layer of cream cheese requires dropping the oven to 350°F. This lets the cream cheese and egg mixture set fully without burning the delicate pastry dough on top.
  • The cooling period: Let the cobbler cool on the counter for at least 30 to 45 minutes before slicing. Cutting it too soon will cause the warm, molten cream cheese layer to run. As it cools, the cream cheese stabilizes into a perfect, sliceable texture.
