Papa Lanc Party Pasta

Papa Lanc’s Party Pasta

1 Box Noodles (Zitti)

2 Jars Spaghetti Sauce

Chopped onions (half whole)

1-2 Lbs. Meat (Jimmy Dean Italian)

Italian Seasoning

4 cups Italian Cheese

Parmesan Cheese

Mushrooms

Black Olives

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Cook Noodles

Brown Meat with Onions

Add Sauce, Noodles, Shrooms, Olives.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Layer 1/3rd sauce

Layer Italian Cheese

Layer 1/3rd Sauce

Layer Italian Cheese

Layer 1/3rd Sauce

Layer Italian Cheese

Layer Parmesan Cheese

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Bake 350, 40 mins covered with foil.
