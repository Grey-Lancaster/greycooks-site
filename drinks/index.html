Hot Chocolate

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups heavy cream
  • 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 6 cups milk
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

Add all the ingredients to a crock pot. Heat until the chips are melted.

Reggie's Margaritas

Ingredients

  • 2–3 parts margarita mix (depending on how strong you want it)
  • 1 part silver Patrón
  • 1 part triple sec

Instructions

Mix and chill for at least 12 hours before serving.

Note: Best enjoyed by a pool on Kiawah Island.
