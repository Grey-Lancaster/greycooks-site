Nannie's Brown Rice

Nannie's original recipe — Celia Wood

Ingredients

  • ½ stick butter, melted
  • 1 cup long-grain parboiled rice, uncooked
  • 1 can French onion soup
  • 1 can beef consommé soup

Instructions

Combine all ingredients. Place in a covered casserole dish. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour.

Bonus tip: Arrange chicken thighs on top before baking. May need to increase cook time by 15 minutes. I actually get skin-on thighs and remove the skin from half of them. Cholesterol adds flavor! 😄
Note: This is Nannie's (Celia Wood) original recipe. Use long-grain parboiled rice — not instant — so it cooks up properly in the hour.

Nannie's Pineapple Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 (20 oz) can chunked pineapple, drained
  • 1 (20 oz) can crushed pineapple, drained
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 3 T pineapple juice
  • 6 T all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, grated
  • ⅓ cup Ritz crackers, crushed
  • ⅓ cup butter, melted

Instructions

Mix the sugar, chunked and crushed pineapple, flour, and cheese together in a large bowl. Pour into a casserole dish.

Combine the crushed crackers with the pineapple juice and melted butter, and spread evenly over the top.

Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
